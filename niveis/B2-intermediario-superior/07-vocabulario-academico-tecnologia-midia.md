## Vocabulário por tema

### 2. Conectores (Linking Words) para redação

| Função | Conectores |
|---|---|
| Adicionar ideia | moreover, furthermore, in addition, also |
| Contrastar | however, on the other hand, nevertheless, although, despite |
| Causa e consequência | therefore, as a result, consequently, due to, because of |
| Exemplificar | for example, for instance, such as |
| Concluir | in conclusion, to sum up, overall, all in all |

> **Although** the results were positive, **further** research is needed. **Moreover**, the sample size was small. **Therefore**, we can't draw definitive conclusions. **In conclusion**, more studies are necessary.

## Estrutura para redação argumentativa (Essay Writing)

1. **Introduction**, apresente o tema e sua tese
2. **Body paragraphs**, um argumento por parágrafo, com evidências e exemplos
3. **Counter-argument** (opcional), reconheça um ponto de vista contrário
4. **Conclusion**, resuma os pontos e reafirme sua opinião

> **Introduction:** Social media has transformed the way we communicate. **However**, it also raises concerns about privacy and mental health.
> **Body:** **For instance**, studies show that excessive use of social media is linked to anxiety. **Moreover**, personal data is often collected without users' full awareness.
> **Conclusion:** **In conclusion**, while social media offers many benefits, its risks **cannot be ignored**.

## Exercícios

**1)** Complete com o conector adequado (however, therefore, for example, in conclusion):

a) Many people work from home nowadays. ▁▁▁, this can lead to isolation.
b) The evidence is clear. ▁▁▁, we must act now.
c) Some jobs, ▁▁▁ teaching and nursing, cannot be automated.
d) ▁▁▁, technology has both benefits and drawbacks.

**2)** Complete com a palavra correta (findings, evidence, headline, influencer):

a) The news ▁▁▁ caught everyone's attention.
b) The ▁▁▁ of the study were surprising.
c) There isn't enough ▁▁▁ to support this theory.
d) She became a famous social media ▁▁▁.

**3)** Traduza:

a) A inteligência artificial está mudando o mercado de trabalho. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
b) É importante analisar os dados com cuidado. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
c) Notícias falsas se espalham rapidamente nas redes sociais. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁

## Gabarito

**1)** a) However; b) Therefore; c) for example / such as; d) In conclusion

**2)** a) headline; b) findings; c) evidence; d) influencer

**3)** a) Artificial intelligence is changing the job market. b) It's important to analyze the data carefully. c) Fake news spreads quickly on social media.

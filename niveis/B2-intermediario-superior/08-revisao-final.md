# B2 · Lição 8: Revisão Final do Nível

## Resumo do nível

- **Third conditional** (arrependimentos) e **mixed conditionals**
- **Wish / if only**
- Revisão de todos os tempos verbais + **used to / would / be used to / get used to**
- **Voz passiva avançada:** reporting verbs, have something done (causativo)
- **Orações relativas** (definidoras e não-definidoras) + discurso indireto avançado
- **Gerúndio x infinitivo** + modais de crítica (should have, needn't have)
- **Phrasal verbs, idioms e collocations**
- Vocabulário acadêmico, tecnologia, mídia + conectores de redação

## Teste geral (20 questões)

**1)** Complete (third conditional): If I ▁▁▁ (know), I ▁▁▁ (tell) you.

**2)** Complete (mixed conditional): If she ▁▁▁ (study) medicine, she ▁▁▁ (be) a doctor now.

**3)** Complete com wish: I ▁▁▁ I ▁▁▁ (not/say) that yesterday.

**4)** Complete: When I was young, I ▁▁▁ (use to/would) collect stamps. *(estado, só uma opção é correta)*

**5)** Complete: I ▁▁▁ (be) used to ▁▁▁ (work) at night, I've done it for years.

**6)** Complete (voz passiva com reporting): People believe he is a genius. → He ▁▁▁ ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁.

**7)** Complete (causativo): She ▁▁▁ (have/her hair/cut) yesterday.

**8)** Complete com pronome relativo: The man ▁▁▁ car was stolen called the police.

**9)** Pontue corretamente (não-definidora): My brother who lives in Canada is a doctor. *(considere que você tem só um irmão)*

**10)** Complete com gerúndio ou infinitivo: She avoided ▁▁▁ (answer) the question.

**11)** Complete: I stopped ▁▁▁ (smoke) three years ago. *(parei de fumar)*

**12)** Complete com should have, shouldn't have ou needn't have: You ▁▁▁ (study) more, you failed.

**13)** Complete o phrasal verb: He ▁▁▁ (turn down) the job offer.

**14)** Complete o idiom: The test was a piece of ▁▁▁.

**15)** Complete: make ou do? I need to ▁▁▁ a decision.

**16)** Traduza: "Se eu tivesse saído mais cedo, eu não teria perdido o voo."

**17)** Traduza: "Estou acostumado a trabalhar sob pressão."

**18)** Traduza: "Diz-se que ele é o melhor engenheiro da empresa."

**19)** Corrija: "I enjoy to read books."

**20)** Escreva um parágrafo curto (4-5 frases) dando sua opinião sobre um tema atual, usando pelo menos 2 conectores (however, therefore, moreover, in conclusion).

## Gabarito

**1)** had known / would have told
**2)** had studied / would be
**3)** wish / hadn't said
**4)** used to (estado, "would" não se aplica)
**5)** am / working
**6)** is believed to be a genius. (ou: It is believed that he is a genius.)
**7)** had her hair cut
**8)** whose
**9)** My brother, who lives in Canada, is a doctor.
**10)** answering
**11)** smoking
**12)** should have studied
**13)** turned down
**14)** cake
**15)** make
**16)** If I had left earlier, I wouldn't have missed the flight.
**17)** I am used to working under pressure.
**18)** It is said that he is the best engineer in the company. / He is said to be the best engineer in the company.
**19)** I enjoy **reading** books.
**20)** Resposta livre, exemplo: "In my opinion, remote work has many benefits. However, it can also lead to isolation. Moreover, not everyone has a proper home office setup. In conclusion, companies should offer flexible options."

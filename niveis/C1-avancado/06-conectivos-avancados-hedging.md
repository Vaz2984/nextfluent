# C1 · Lição 6: Conectivos Avançados e Hedging Language

## Objetivos da lição

- Usar conectivos sofisticados para argumentação
- Usar "hedging" (linguagem de cautela) para soar mais natural e diplomático
- Praticar discourse markers usados por falantes fluentes

## Explicação

### 1. O que é "hedging"?

**Hedging** é a arte de suavizar afirmações para soar menos categórico, mais educado e mais acadêmico, falantes nativos fluentes raramente afirmam algo de forma 100% absoluta. É uma marca importante de fluência em C1.

| Direto/absoluto | Com hedging (mais natural/diplomático) |
|---|---|
| This is wrong. | This **doesn't seem entirely** accurate. / This **might not be** correct. |
| You should do this. | You **might want to consider** doing this. |
| This will happen. | This **is likely to** happen. / This **could potentially** happen. |
| I disagree. | I **tend to disagree**, though I see your point. |
| That's a bad idea. | I'm **not entirely convinced** that's the best approach. |

**Estruturas comuns de hedging:**
- It **could be argued** that...
- **It seems/appears** that...
- **There is a possibility** that...
- **To some extent**, this is true.
- **Arguably**, this is the best option.
- This **tends to** happen when...
- **It's worth noting** that...

### 2. Conectivos avançados (Advanced Discourse Markers)

| Função | Conectivos avançados |
|---|---|
| Adicionar/reforçar | not only... but also, what's more, on top of that |
| Contrastar (forte) | be that as it may, that said, notwithstanding, albeit |
| Causa | given that, on the grounds that, in light of |
| Consequência | hence, thus, as a consequence, in turn |
| Concessão | granted that, admittedly, while it's true that |
| Resumir/concluir | all things considered, in essence, ultimately |

> **Given that** resources are limited, **it could be argued** the project should be postponed. **That said**, delaying it further **might** damage client relationships. **All things considered**, a partial rollout **seems** to be the most balanced solution.

### 3. Albeit, whereby, notwithstanding: conectivos de registro muito formal

> The plan was approved, **albeit** with some reservations.
> → O plano foi aprovado, ainda que com algumas ressalvas.
> They created a system **whereby** employees can report issues anonymously.
> → Eles criaram um sistema pelo qual funcionários podem reportar problemas anonimamente.
> **Notwithstanding** the risks, the company decided to proceed.
> → Não obstante os riscos, a empresa decidiu prosseguir.

### 4. Advérbios de hedging (modificadores de grau)

somewhat (um tanto), fairly (razoavelmente), relatively (relativamente), rather (bastante/meio), arguably (indiscutivelmente/pode-se argumentar), presumably (presumivelmente), apparently (aparentemente)

> The results were **somewhat** surprising. → Os resultados foram um tanto surpreendentes.
> This is **arguably** the best solution available. → Esta é, pode-se argumentar, a melhor solução disponível.

## Vocabulário

diplomacy (diplomacia), nuanced (com nuances), categorical (categórico/absoluto)

## Prática

> **It could be argued that** remote work **tends to** increase productivity, **although** this **may not** apply to every industry. **Admittedly**, some roles require in-person collaboration. **That said**, **it's worth noting** that flexibility **arguably** improves employee retention. **All things considered**, a hybrid model **seems** to offer the best balance.

## Exercícios

**1)** Reescreva as frases usando hedging (deixe menos categórico):

a) This is the best solution. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
b) You should quit your job. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
c) This will fail. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁

**2)** Complete com o conectivo adequado (albeit, notwithstanding, whereby, that said):

a) The plan worked, ▁▁▁ with a few delays.
b) ▁▁▁ the difficulties, they finished the project on time.
c) They designed a process ▁▁▁ customers can request refunds easily.
d) It's risky. ▁▁▁, it could pay off.

**3)** Traduza usando hedging:

a) Isso parece ser a melhor opção. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
b) Pode-se argumentar que essa política é injusta. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
c) Vale a pena notar que os resultados variam. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁

## Gabarito

**1)** a) This seems/appears to be the best solution. b) You might want to consider quitting your job. c) This is likely to fail. / This could potentially fail.

**2)** a) albeit; b) Notwithstanding; c) whereby; d) That said

**3)** a) This seems to be the best option. b) It could be argued that this policy is unfair. c) It's worth noting that the results vary.

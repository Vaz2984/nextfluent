C1 · Lição 3: Collocations Avançadas e Expressões Idiomáticas

Objetivos da lição

  • Ampliar collocations de nível avançado
  • Aprender idioms usados em contextos profissionais e formais
  • Aprender expressões para debate e negociação

Explicação

1. Collocations acadêmicas e profissionais

Collocation Tradução
reach a consensus chegar a um consenso
draw a parallel traçar um paralelo
play a crucial role desempenhar um papel crucial
bridge the gap reduzir/eliminar a lacuna
pose a threat/challenge representar uma ameaça/desafio
raise awareness aumentar a conscientização
strike a balance encontrar um equilíbrio
gain momentum ganhar força/impulso
set a precedent abrir um precedente
yield results gerar resultados
tackle an issue enfrentar/lidar com um problema
foster growth fomentar o crescimento

The new policy poses a threat to small businesses. → A nova política representa uma ameaça para pequenas empresas. The two sides finally reached a consensus after hours of debate. It's important to strike a balance between work and personal life.

2. Idioms de nível avançado

Idiom Significado Exemplo
a double-edged sword uma faca de dois gumes Social media is a double-edged sword.
the tip of the iceberg apenas a ponta do iceberg This scandal is just the tip of the iceberg.
read between the lines ler nas entrelinhas You need to read between the lines of that email.
jump on the bandwagon entrar na onda/moda Many companies jumped on the bandwagon of AI.
back to square one voltar à estaca zero The deal fell through, we're back to square one.
food for thought algo para se refletir Her speech gave us a lot of food for thought.
a grey area uma zona cinzenta (ambígua) The law on this topic is a grey area.
take something with a grain of salt não levar algo 100% a sério You should take his advice with a grain of salt.
the elephant in the room o assunto óbvio que ninguém menciona No one addressed the elephant in the room.
cut corners economizar/fazer com menos qualidade The company cut corners on safety.

3. Expressões para debate e argumentação formal

Expressão Uso
To play devil's advocate... Para fazer o papel de advogado do diabo (contra-argumentar de propósito)
That being said... Dito isso...
To put it another way... Colocando de outra forma...
It could be argued that... Poder-se-ia argumentar que...
There's no denying that... Não há como negar que...
I take your point, but... Eu entendo seu ponto, mas...
At the end of the day... No final das contas...

To play devil's advocate, isn't it also true that regulation could stifle innovation? There's no denying that climate change is accelerating. That being said, solutions must be economically viable.

Vocabulário

nuance (nuance/sutileza), rhetoric (retórica), viable (viável), stifle (sufocar/reprimir)

Prática: Debate formal

It could be argued that remote work poses a challenge to company culture. However, there's no denying that it also fosters better work-life balance. To put it another way, the shift to remote work is a double-edged sword, and companies must strike a balance between flexibility and collaboration.

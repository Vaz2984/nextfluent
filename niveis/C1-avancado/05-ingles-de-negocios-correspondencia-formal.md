# C1 · Lição 5: Inglês de Negócios e Correspondência Formal

## Objetivos da lição

- Escrever e-mails profissionais em inglês
- Participar de reuniões e negociações em inglês
- Aprender vocabulário corporativo essencial

## Explicação

### 1. Estrutura de um e-mail formal

| Parte | Exemplos de frases |
|---|---|
| Saudação | Dear Mr. Smith, / Dear Ms. Johnson, / To whom it may concern, |
| Abertura | I am writing to inquire about... / I am writing regarding... / Further to our conversation... |
| Corpo | Apresente o motivo com clareza e objetividade |
| Pedido | I would appreciate it if you could... / Could you please...? / I would be grateful if... |
| Fechamento | Please let me know if you need any further information. / I look forward to hearing from you. |
| Despedida | Best regards, / Kind regards, / Sincerely, |

**Exemplo completo:**
> Dear Ms. Taylor,
>
> I am writing to follow up on our meeting last week regarding the new contract terms. I would appreciate it if you could send the updated document by Friday.
>
> Please let me know if you need any further information.
>
> Best regards,
> Carlos Mendes

### 2. Expressões úteis para reuniões (Meetings)

| Situação | Expressão |
|---|---|
| Abrir a reunião | Let's get started. / Shall we begin? |
| Apresentar a pauta | Today, we're going to discuss... / The main goal of this meeting is... |
| Pedir opinião | What are your thoughts on this? / Would you like to add anything? |
| Concordar | I couldn't agree more. / That's a valid point. |
| Discordar educadamente | I see your point, but I have a different view. / With all due respect, I disagree. |
| Interromper educadamente | Sorry to interrupt, but... / If I may just add something... |
| Encerrar | Let's wrap up. / To summarize what we've discussed... |

### 3. Negociação (Negotiation)

| Expressão | Uso |
|---|---|
| Let's find a middle ground. | Vamos encontrar um meio-termo. |
| That's not quite what we had in mind. | Não é bem o que tínhamos em mente. |
| We're open to negotiation. | Estamos abertos a negociar. |
| Under what conditions would you...? | Sob quais condições você...? |
| Let's put this on hold for now. | Vamos deixar isso em espera por agora. |
| We need to reach a win-win solution. | Precisamos chegar a uma solução ganha-ganha. |

## Vocabulário: Verbos formais de e-mail

I am writing to... (escrevo para...), I would like to inform you that... (gostaria de informá-lo(a) de que...), please find attached... (segue em anexo...), should you have any questions... (caso tenha alguma dúvida...)

## Prática: E-mail de negociação

> Dear Mr. Alves,
>
> Further to our call yesterday, I would like to propose a revised timeline for the project deliverables. Given the current budget constraints, we believe a phased approach would allow us to reach a win-win solution for both parties.
>
> Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.
>
> Kind regards,
> Fernanda Costa

## Exercícios

**1)** Complete o e-mail formal:

a) Dear Mr. Johnson, I am writing ▁▁▁ inquire about the status of our order.
b) I would ▁▁▁ it if you could send the report by Monday.
c) I look forward to ▁▁▁ from you.

**2)** Complete com a expressão de reunião correta (couldn't agree more, sorry to interrupt, let's wrap up):

a) ▁▁▁, but I have an important point to add.
b) I ▁▁▁, that's exactly what I was thinking.
c) We're almost out of time, so ▁▁▁.

**3)** Traduza:

a) Segue em anexo o relatório solicitado. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
b) Precisamos terceirizar essa parte do projeto. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
c) Vamos encontrar um meio-termo. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁

## Gabarito

**1)** a) to; b) appreciate; c) hearing

**2)** a) Sorry to interrupt; b) couldn't agree more; c) let's wrap up

**3)** a) Please find attached the requested report. b) We need to outsource this part of the project. c) Let's find a middle ground.

C1 · Lição 1: Inversão e Estruturas de Ênfase

Objetivos da lição

  • Usar inversão (inversion) para dar ênfase formal/literária
  • Usar cleft sentences (it is... that / what... is) para destacar informação
  • Reconhecer esses recursos em textos formais e discursos

Explicação

1. Inversão com advérbios negativos

Quando uma frase começa com certos advérbios negativos/restritivos, a ordem sujeito-verbo se inverte (como em perguntas) para dar ênfase formal e dramática, muito comum em escrita formal, discursos e literatura:

Advérbio Exemplo
Never Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.
Rarely Rarely does she complain about her job.
Not only... but also Not only did he apologize, but he also offered to pay for the damage.
No sooner...than No sooner had I sat down than the phone rang.
Hardly...when Hardly had she arrived when the meeting started.
Little Little did he know that he would become famous.
Only after/then Only after the accident did he realize the risk.
Under no circumstances Under no circumstances should you share this password.

Frase normal: I have never seen such a beautiful sunset. Com inversão (mais enfática/formal): Never have I seen such a beautiful sunset.

2. Cleft Sentences: estruturas de ênfase

Usadas para destacar uma parte específica da frase.

It-cleft: It is/was + elemento destacado + that/who...

It was Maria who organized the whole event. → Foi a Maria quem organizou todo o evento. (destaca "Maria") It wasn't until 2020 that the company started exporting. → Só em 2020 é que a empresa começou a exportar.

Wh-cleft (What-cleft): What + oração + is/was + elemento destacado

What I really need is some rest. → O que eu realmente preciso é de descanso. What surprised me most was his honesty. → O que mais me surpreendeu foi a honestidade dele.

3. Inversão em condicionais formais (sem "if")

Em registro formal/escrito, é possível omitir "if" e inverter a ordem:

Com if (neutro) Sem if (formal, invertido)
If I were you... Were I you...
If you should need help... Should you need help...
If she had known... Had she known...

Had I known about the traffic, I would have left earlier. → Se eu soubesse do trânsito, eu teria saído mais cedo. (equivalente formal a "If I had known") Should you have any questions, feel free to contact us. → Caso tenha alguma dúvida, sinta-se à vontade para nos contatar. (comum em e-mails formais)

Vocabulário: Registro formal

nonetheless (não obstante), whereby (pelo qual), thereby (assim/dessa forma), henceforth (doravante)

Prática: Trecho formal

Never before had the company faced such a crisis. Not only did sales drop, but costs also rose sharply. Had the board acted sooner, the outcome might have been different. It was the CEO's decision that ultimately saved the business.

Exercícios

1) Reescreva usando inversão:

a) I have never seen such chaos. → b) She rarely goes out on weekdays. → c) If you need anything, call me. → (sem "if")

2) Reescreva com cleft sentences, destacando a parte em negrito:

a) John broke the window. → It was b) I need more time, not money. → What

3) Traduza:

a) Jamais vi algo tão impressionante. (com inversão) → b) Foi você quem me ajudou naquele dia. → c) Caso precise de ajuda, não hesite em ligar. →

Gabarito

1) a) Never have I seen such chaos. b) Rarely does she go out on weekdays. c) Should you need anything, call me.

2) a) It was John who broke the window., b) What I need is more time, not money.

3) a) Never have I seen something so impressive. b) It was you who helped me that day. c) Should you need help, don't hesitate to call.

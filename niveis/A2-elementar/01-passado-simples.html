A2 · Lição 1: Passado Simples (Simple Past)

Objetivos da lição

  • Usar o passado simples com verbos regulares e irregulares
  • Fazer frases afirmativas, negativas e interrogativas no passado
  • Aprender expressões de tempo no passado

Explicação

O passado simples é usado para ações completas e concluídas no passado.

I watched a movie yesterday. → Eu assisti a um filme ontem.

1. Verbos regulares: adicionar -ed

Regra Exemplo
Geral: + ed work → worked
Termina em "e": + d live → lived
Consoante + y: y → ied study → studied
Vogal curta + consoante: dobra a consoante stop → stopped

2. Verbos irregulares

Não seguem regra, precisam ser decorados. Alguns dos mais comuns:

Presente Passado Tradução
go went ir
have had ter
do did fazer
see saw ver
eat ate comer
make made fazer/produzir
take took pegar
come came vir
get got conseguir/pegar
know knew saber
think thought pensar
say said dizer
buy bought comprar
write wrote escrever
read read (pronúncia muda: "red") ler

Veja a lista completa em recursos/verbos-irregulares.md

3. Estruturas

Afirmativa: sujeito + verbo no passado (igual para todos os pronomes!)

I worked. She worked. They worked. I went. She went. They went.

Negativa: sujeito + didn't + verbo no infinitivo (sem -ed!)

I didn't work yesterday. → Eu não trabalhei ontem. She didn't go to the party. → Ela não foi à festa.

Interrogativa: Did + sujeito + verbo no infinitivo?

Did you watch the game? → Você assistiu ao jogo? Did she go to school? → Ela foi à escola?

Respostas curtas: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

4. Passado do verbo to be

Pronome Passado
I / He / She / It was
You / We / They were

I was at home. / They weren't ready. / Was she happy?

Vocabulário: Expressões de tempo no passado

Expressão Tradução
yesterday ontem
last night/week/month/year ontem à noite / semana/mês/ano passado
ago (two days ago) atrás (dois dias atrás)
in 2020 em 2020
when I was a child quando eu era criança

Diálogo modelo

A: Did you have a good weekend? B: Yes, I did! I went to the beach with my family. What about you? A: I stayed home. I watched movies and read a book. B: Sounds relaxing!

Exercícios

1) Complete com o passado dos verbos:

a) She (go) to Paris last year. b) They (not/study) for the test. c) you (see) that movie? d) He (buy) a new car. e) I (be) very tired last night.

2) Transforme em negativa:

a) I watched the game. → b) She went to work. →

3) Transforme em pergunta:

a) They lived in London. → b) He had a dog. →

4) Traduza:

a) Eu comi pizza ontem à noite. → b) Ela não foi ao trabalho ontem. → c) Você viu o filme novo? →

Gabarito

1) a) went; b) didn't study; c) Did / see; d) bought; e) was

2) a) I didn't watch the game., b) She didn't go to work.

3) a) Did they live in London?, b) Did he have a dog?

4) a) I ate pizza last night. b) She didn't go to work yesterday. c) Did you see the new movie?

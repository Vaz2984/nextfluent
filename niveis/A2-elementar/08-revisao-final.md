# A2 · Lição 8: Revisão Final do Nível

## Resumo do nível

- **Passado simples** (regular e irregular) + passado do verbo to be
- **Passado contínuo** e combinação com passado simples (when/while)
- **Comparativos e superlativos** + as...as
- **Futuro:** will x going to + presente contínuo com sentido de futuro
- **Modais:** must, mustn't, have to, don't have to, should
- **Contáveis/incontáveis:** some, any, much, many, a lot of, a few, a little
- **Preposições** de tempo (in/on/at) e movimento
- **Phrasal verbs** básicos do dia a dia
- Vocabulário de viagem, trabalho e compras

## Teste geral (20 questões)

**1)** Complete no passado: Yesterday, I ▁▁▁ (go) to the market and ▁▁▁ (buy) some fruit.

**2)** Complete: While she ▁▁▁ (cook), he ▁▁▁ (call) her.

**3)** Complete com comparativo: This phone is ▁▁▁ (good) than my old one.

**4)** Complete com superlativo: She is ▁▁▁ (tall) student in the class.

**5)** Escolha will ou going to: I already bought the ticket. I ▁▁▁ (travel) next week.

**6)** Escolha will ou going to: Look at those dark clouds! It ▁▁▁ (rain).

**7)** Complete: You ▁▁▁ (mustn't/don't have to) smoke here, it's forbidden.

**8)** Complete: You ▁▁▁ (should/shouldn't) see a doctor if you feel sick.

**9)** Complete: I don't have ▁▁▁ (much/many) money left.

**10)** Complete: There are ▁▁▁ (a few/a little) apples in the fridge.

**11)** Complete a preposição: The meeting is ▁▁▁ Monday ▁▁▁ 9 am.

**12)** Complete o phrasal verb: Please ▁▁▁ off the lights before leaving. (turn)

**13)** Traduza: "Ela estava dormindo quando eu cheguei em casa."

**14)** Traduza: "Nós vamos viajar para a praia no próximo mês." (plano já decidido)

**15)** Traduza: "Você não precisa trazer nada."

**16)** Traduza: "Há muitas pessoas no evento."

**17)** Corrija: "She don't have to go if she no want."

**18)** Corrija: "I have much friends in Brazil."

**19)** Complete o diálogo: A: How ▁▁▁ is this jacket? B: It's $30.

**20)** Escreva 3 frases sobre o que você fez no fim de semana passado (passado simples).

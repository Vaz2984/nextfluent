A2 · Lição 6: Preposições de Tempo e Phrasal Verbs Básicos

Objetivos da lição

  • Usar preposições de tempo (in, on, at)
  • Usar preposições de movimento
  • Aprender os phrasal verbs mais comuns do dia a dia

Explicação

1. Preposições de tempo: IN, ON, AT

Preposição Uso Exemplo
at horas exatas, momentos específicos at 7 o'clock, at night, at lunch, at the moment
on dias e datas on Monday, on July 5th, on my birthday
in meses, anos, estações, períodos longos in July, in 2024, in summer, in the morning

I wake up at 7 am. I have a meeting on Monday. I was born in 1995.

2. Preposições de movimento

Preposição Tradução Exemplo
to para (destino) I'm going to the store.
from de (origem) I'm from Brazil.
into para dentro de She walked into the room.
out of para fora de He came out of the house.
through através de We walked through the park.
across através de/do outro lado She swam across the river.
along ao longo de They walked along the beach.

3. Phrasal verbs (verbo + preposição/partícula = novo significado)

Phrasal verbs são combinações de verbo + partícula que criam um significado diferente do verbo original. São essenciais no inglês do dia a dia!

Phrasal verb Significado Exemplo
wake up acordar I wake up at 7 am.
get up levantar (da cama) I get up right after waking up.
get on/off entrar/sair (de ônibus, trem) We got on the bus.
get in/out (of) entrar/sair (de carro) She got out of the car.
turn on/off ligar/desligar Please turn off the lights.
look for procurar I'm looking for my keys.
look after cuidar de She looks after her little brother.
pick up pegar / buscar alguém Can you pick up the kids?
put on vestir/colocar He put on his jacket.
take off tirar (roupa) / decolar (avião) She took off her shoes.
give up desistir Don't give up!
find out descobrir I found out the truth.
grow up crescer I grew up in Brazil.
come back voltar He came back late.
go on continuar Please, go on with your story.
fill in/out preencher (formulário) Please fill in this form.

Muitos phrasal verbs são "separáveis", o objeto pode ficar entre o verbo e a partícula: turn off the lights = turn the lights off, mas com pronomes é obrigatório separar: turn them off (nunca "turn off them").

Vocabulário: Viagem (Travel)

Palavra Tradução
airport aeroporto
flight voo
passport passaporte
luggage/baggage bagagem
ticket passagem
check-in check-in
boarding pass cartão de embarque
customs alfândega
destination destino

Diálogo modelo

A: What time does your flight leave? B: It leaves at 3 pm on Friday. I need to check in two hours before. A: Don't forget to turn off your phone during boarding! Who's going to pick you up at the airport? B: My brother. He'll be looking for me at the arrivals gate.

Exercícios

1) Complete com in, on ou at:

a) My birthday is May. b) The meeting is 3 pm. c) We're going to the beach Saturday. d) I was born 1998.

2) Complete com o phrasal verb adequado (wake up, look for, turn off, take off, give up):

a) Please the TV before you sleep. b) I always at 6:30. c) She never s, she always finishes what she starts. d) He his shoes before entering the house. e) I'm my glasses, have you seen them?

3) Traduza:

a) Ela cuida do irmão mais novo. → b) Eu cresci em São Paulo. → c) Você pode me buscar às 5? →

Gabarito

1) a) in; b) at; c) on; d) in

2) a) turn off; b) wake up; c) gives up; d) takes off; e) looking for

3) a) She looks after her little brother. b) I grew up in São Paulo. c) Can you pick me up at 5?

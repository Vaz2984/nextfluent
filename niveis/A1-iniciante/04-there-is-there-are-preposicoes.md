# A1 · Lição 4: There is / There are e Preposições de Lugar

## Objetivos da lição

- Usar **there is / there are** para dizer que algo existe
- Usar preposições de lugar
- Aprender vocabulário da casa

## Explicação

### 1. There is / There are

Usamos para dizer que algo **existe** ou **está presente** em um lugar.

| Singular / incontável | Plural |
|---|---|
| **There is** (There's) | **There are** |

**Afirmativa:**
> **There is** a book on the table. → Há um livro na mesa.
> **There are** three chairs in the room. → Há três cadeiras na sala.

**Negativa:**
> **There isn't** a TV in my room. → Não há TV no meu quarto.
> **There aren't** any pictures on the wall. → Não há fotos na parede.

**Interrogativa:**
> **Is there** a bathroom here? → Há um banheiro aqui?
> **Are there** any students in the class? → Há alunos na classe?

**Respostas curtas:**
> Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
> Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.

> **some** é usado em frases afirmativas (there are **some** apples) e **any** em negativas/interrogativas (there aren't **any** apples / are there **any** apples?)

### 2. Preposições de lugar (Prepositions of Place)

| Preposição | Tradução | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| in | dentro de / em | The keys are **in** the bag. |
| on | sobre/em cima de | The book is **on** the table. |
| under | embaixo de | The cat is **under** the bed. |
| next to | ao lado de | The chair is **next to** the door. |
| between | entre | The bank is **between** the school and the market. |
| in front of | na frente de | The car is **in front of** the house. |
| behind | atrás de | The garden is **behind** the house. |
| near | perto de | The park is **near** my house. |

## Vocabulário: A casa (The House)

| Palavra | Tradução |
|---|---|
| living room | sala de estar |
| bedroom | quarto |
| kitchen | cozinha |
| bathroom | banheiro |
| garden | jardim |
| garage | garagem |
| wall | parede |
| floor | chão/andar |
| ceiling | teto |
| roof | telhado |

## Prática

> There is a big kitchen **in** my house. There are two bathrooms. The living room is **next to** the kitchen, and there is a garden **behind** the house.

> Is there a table in the kitchen? Yes, there is. It's **in front of** the window.

## Exercícios

**1)** Complete com there is / there are / there isn't / there aren't:

a) ▁▁▁ a computer on the desk.
b) ▁▁▁ five books in my bag.
c) ▁▁▁ any milk in the fridge. (negativo)
d) ▁▁▁ a garden in my house.

**2)** Complete com a preposição correta (in, on, under, next to, between):

a) The cat is ▁▁▁ the table (embaixo de).
b) The pen is ▁▁▁ the notebook (sobre).
c) The bank is ▁▁▁ the pharmacy and the school (entre).
d) My phone is ▁▁▁ my bag (dentro de).

**3)** Transforme em pergunta:

a) There is a park near here. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
b) There are two dogs in the garden. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁

**4)** Traduza:

a) Há um sofá na sala. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
b) Não há cadeiras na cozinha. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
c) Há um banheiro perto daqui? → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁

## Gabarito

**1)** a) There is; b) There are; c) There isn't; d) There is

**2)** a) under; b) on; c) between; d) in

**3)** a) Is there a park near here?, b) Are there two dogs in the garden?

**4)** a) There is a sofa in the living room. b) There aren't (any) chairs in the kitchen. c) Is there a bathroom near here?

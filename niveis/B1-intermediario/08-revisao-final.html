B1 · Lição 8: Revisão Final do Nível

Resumo do nível

  • Present perfect (experiência, resultado no presente) + for/since + already/yet/just/ever/never
  • Present perfect continuous (duração) e past perfect (passado do passado)
  • Condicionais: zero, first (real) e second (hipotética)
  • Voz passiva em vários tempos verbais
  • Discurso indireto (reported speech): afirmações, perguntas, ordens
  • Modais de dedução: must/might/could/can't (presente e passado)
  • Phrasal verbs intermediários
  • Vocabulário: saúde, negócios, meio ambiente + expressar opinião

Teste geral (20 questões)

1) Complete: I (never/be) to Australia.

2) Complete: She (live) in this city for ten years (e ainda mora).

3) Complete: When I got there, the movie (already/start).

4) Complete (first conditional): If it (rain), we (stay) home.

5) Complete (second conditional): If I (be) rich, I (travel) the world.

6) Transforme para a voz passiva: "They built this house in 2000."

7) Transforme para discurso indireto: "I am tired," she said.

8) Transforme para discurso indireto: "Where do you live?" he asked.

9) Complete com must, might ou can't: He's not here. He have left already. (dedução forte)

10) Complete: She looks upset. Something (must/happen).

11) Complete o phrasal verb: We out of milk. (run)

12) Complete: I'm looking forward to (see) you.

13) Traduza: "Eu moro aqui há cinco anos."

14) Traduza: "Se eu fosse você, eu falaria com ela."

15) Traduza: "A carta foi enviada ontem."

16) Traduza: "Ele disse que estava ocupado."

17) Corrija: "If it will rain, I will stay home."

18) Corrija: "He told that he was busy."

19) Complete: In my (opinion), we should recycle more.

20) Escreva 3 frases usando present perfect sobre suas experiências de vida (viagens, comidas, filmes etc).

Gabarito

1) have never been 2) has lived 3) had already started 4) rains / will stay 5) were / would travel 6) This house was built in 2000. 7) She said (that) she was tired. 8) He asked where I lived. 9) must 10) must have happened 11) ran / are running 12) seeing 13) I have lived here for five years. 14) If I were you, I would talk to her. 15) The letter was sent yesterday. 16) He said (that) he was busy. 17) If it rains, I will stay home. 18) He said that he was busy. / He told me that he was busy. 19) opinion 20) Resposta livre, exemplo: "I have visited three countries. I have never tried Japanese food. I have watched that movie twice."

Critério de avanço

  • 18-20 acertos → Excelente! Vá para o nível B2.
  • 14-17 acertos → Revise os pontos que errou.
  • abaixo de 14 → Refaça as lições 1 a 7 com calma.

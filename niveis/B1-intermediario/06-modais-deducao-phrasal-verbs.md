# B1 · Lição 6: Modais de Dedução e Phrasal Verbs Intermediários

## Objetivos da lição

- Usar modais para expressar certeza, possibilidade e dedução
- Ampliar o repertório de phrasal verbs

## Explicação

### 1. Modais de dedução no presente

Usamos modais para dizer o quão **certos** estamos de algo, sem afirmar com 100% de certeza:

| Modal | Grau de certeza | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **must** | quase certeza (positiva) | She **must be** tired, she worked 12 hours. |
| **might / may / could** | possibilidade | He **might be** at home. / It **could be** true. |
| **can't** | quase certeza (negativa) | That **can't be** true! It's impossible. |

> Repare: usamos **must** para dedução positiva forte e **can't** para dedução negativa forte, nunca "mustn't" nesse sentido (mustn't = proibição, não dedução).

> The lights are off. They **must be** out. → As luzes estão apagadas. Eles devem estar fora.
> She **might come** to the party, I'm not sure. → Ela pode vir à festa, não tenho certeza.
> He **can't be** at work, I just saw him at the mall! → Ele não pode estar no trabalho, acabei de vê-lo no shopping!

### 2. Modais de dedução no passado

**Estrutura:** modal + have + particípio passado

| Modal | Exemplo |
|---|---|
| must have + p.p. | She **must have forgotten** her phone. (quase certeza) |
| might/may/could have + p.p. | He **might have missed** the bus. (possibilidade) |
| can't have + p.p. | They **can't have arrived** yet, it's too early. (quase certeza negativa) |

> Where's John? He **must have left** already. → Onde está o John? Ele deve ter saído já.
> She looks upset. Something **might have happened**. → Ela parece chateada. Algo pode ter acontecido.

### 3. Phrasal verbs intermediários

| Phrasal verb | Significado | Exemplo |
|---|---|---|
| carry on | continuar | Please **carry on** with your work. |
| come across | encontrar por acaso | I **came across** an old photo. |
| put off | adiar | We **put off** the meeting until Friday. |
| run out (of) | ficar sem | We **ran out of** milk. |
| set up | montar/estabelecer | They **set up** a new business. |
| break down | quebrar (máquina/carro) | My car **broke down** on the highway. |
| bring up | criar (um filho) / trazer à tona (assunto) | She **brought up** an interesting point. |
| deal with | lidar com | I need to **deal with** this problem. |
| figure out | descobrir/entender | I can't **figure out** how this works. |
| get along (with) | se dar bem com | I **get along** well **with** my coworkers. |
| get over | superar | It took time to **get over** the breakup. |
| look forward to | ansiar por/esperar ansiosamente | I'm **looking forward to** the trip. |
| point out | apontar/destacar | She **pointed out** a mistake in the report. |
| show up | aparecer | He didn't **show up** for the meeting. |
| turn out | acabar sendo/revelar-se | The movie **turned out** to be great. |

> "look forward to" é seguido de **substantivo ou verbo+ing**, nunca infinitivo: *I look forward to **seeing** you* (não "to see").

## Vocabulário: Emoções e reações

worried (preocupado), relieved (aliviado), confused (confuso), surprised (surpreso), disappointed (decepcionado), frustrated (frustrado)

## Diálogo modelo

> **A:** Where's Marcos? He hasn't shown up yet.
> **B:** He must have gotten stuck in traffic. He texted me saying his car broke down last week, so he might have had car trouble again.
> **A:** That's too bad. Well, let's carry on with the meeting without him.
> **B:** Good idea. We can't put it off any longer, we're running out of time!

## Exercícios

**1)** Complete com must, might, could ou can't:

a) She's not answering. She ▁▁▁ be busy. (possibilidade)
b) He ▁▁▁ be at the office, I just saw him leave five minutes ago. (certeza negativa)
c) They ▁▁▁ be tired after such a long trip. (quase certeza)

**2)** Complete com o modal de dedução no passado:

a) She looks exhausted. She ▁▁▁ (must/not sleep) well.
b) I don't see his car. He ▁▁▁ (might/leave) already.

**3)** Complete com o phrasal verb correto (run out of, look forward to, figure out, get along with):

a) I ▁▁▁ seeing you next week!
b) We ▁▁▁ milk, can you buy some?
c) I can't ▁▁▁ this puzzle.
d) She ▁▁▁ her new coworkers very well.

**4)** Traduza:

a) Ela deve estar cansada. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
b) Ele deve ter esquecido a reunião. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
c) Estou ansioso para as férias. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁

## Gabarito

**1)** a) might; b) can't; c) must

**2)** a) must not have slept, b) might have left

**3)** a) look forward to; b) ran out of / are running out of; c) figure out; d) gets along with

**4)** a) She must be tired. b) He must have forgotten the meeting. c) I'm looking forward to the vacation.

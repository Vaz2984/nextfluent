# Trabalho · Lição 7: Negociação e Feedback

## Objetivos da lição

- Negociar salário e condições de trabalho
- Dar feedback construtivo em inglês
- Receber críticas e feedback profissionalmente

## Explicação

### 1. Negociando salário (Salary Negotiation)

**Quando perguntam sua expectativa salarial, evite dar um número exato logo de cara:**

> "Based on my research and experience, I'm looking for something in the range of $X to $Y."
> "I'd like to learn more about the role and the full compensation package before discussing numbers."
> "What is the budget you have in mind for this position?" *(devolver a pergunta é uma tática válida)*

**Negociando uma proposta:**
> "Thank you for the offer. I'm very excited about this opportunity. Based on my experience and the market rate, I was hoping we could discuss a salary closer to $X."
> "Is there any flexibility on the base salary?"
> "Besides the salary, I'd like to ask about other benefits, such as remote work flexibility and professional development budget."

**Aceitando ou recusando uma proposta:**
> "I'm happy to accept the offer as it stands."
> "I appreciate the offer, but I've decided to pursue another opportunity that's a better fit at this time."

### 2. Negociando prazos e escopo de projeto

> "Given the current scope, I don't think this deadline is realistic. Could we discuss an extension?"
> "We could meet the original deadline if we reduce the scope slightly."
> "What's the flexibility on this timeline?"

### 3. Dando feedback construtivo (Giving Feedback)

O modelo **"Sandwich"** (elogio - crítica - elogio/incentivo) é clássico, mas evite exagerar, feedback direto e específico é mais valorizado em muitas culturas corporativas modernas:

**Estrutura eficaz:**
1. Seja específico sobre o comportamento/resultado (não a pessoa)
2. Explique o impacto
3. Sugira uma melhoria concreta

> "I noticed the report was submitted a few days late, which pushed back the whole team's timeline. Going forward, could you flag any delays earlier so we can adjust?"
> "Your presentation was really clear and well-organized. One suggestion: adding a summary slide at the end could help reinforce the key points."

**Frases úteis para dar feedback:**
> I wanted to share some feedback on...
> Something I think could be improved is...
> One area of opportunity is...
> What worked really well was...

### 4. Recebendo feedback (Receiving Feedback)

Mostrar abertura, mesmo com críticas, é uma habilidade profissional valiosa:

> "Thank you for the feedback, I really appreciate it."
> "That's helpful, I hadn't thought of it that way."
> "Could you give me an example so I can understand better?"
> "I'll definitely work on that going forward."

**Se você discordar do feedback (com respeito):**
> "I see your point, but I'd like to share some context that might explain the situation."
> "I understand the concern, can we discuss possible solutions together?"

## Vocabulário: Negociação e feedback

| to flag something | sinalizar algo |
| counteroffer | contraproposta |

## Diálogo modelo: Negociação salarial

> **Recruiter:** We'd like to offer you the position, with a starting salary of $60,000.
> **Candidate:** Thank you so much, I'm really excited about this opportunity. Based on my experience and market research, I was hoping we could discuss something closer to $68,000.
> **Recruiter:** Let me check with the team, is there anything else you'd like to include in the discussion?
> **Candidate:** Yes, I'd also love to understand the flexibility around remote work days.

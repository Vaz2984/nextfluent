# Trabalho · Lição 4: Reuniões e Apresentações

## Objetivos da lição

- Participar ativamente de reuniões em inglês
- Estruturar e conduzir uma apresentação profissional
- Lidar com perguntas e imprevistos durante uma apresentação

## Explicação

### 1. Frases essenciais para reuniões

| Situação | Frase |
|---|---|
| Abrir a reunião | Thanks everyone for joining. Let's get started. |
| Apresentar a pauta | Today's agenda includes three main points... |
| Passar a palavra | I'll hand it over to Marcos to walk us through the numbers. |
| Pedir para repetir | Sorry, could you say that again? / Could you clarify that? |
| Concordar | That makes sense. / I'm on board with that. |
| Discordar educadamente | I see it a bit differently. / I'm not sure I fully agree. |
| Pedir tempo para pensar | Can I get back to you on that? / Let me think about it. |
| Redirecionar a conversa | Let's circle back to that point later. / Let's stay focused on the main topic. |
| Resumir decisões | So, to summarize, we've agreed on... / Let's recap the action items. |
| Encerrar | I think that covers everything. Thanks for your time. |

### 2. Estrutura de uma apresentação (Presentation)

| Parte | O que fazer | Frases-modelo |
|---|---|---|
| **Abertura** | Apresente-se e o tema | "Good morning, everyone. Today I'll be talking about..." |
| **Visão geral** | Diga a estrutura da apresentação | "I've divided this presentation into three parts: first... then... and finally..." |
| **Desenvolvimento** | Apresente o conteúdo com transições claras | "Let's move on to the next point." / "This brings us to..." |
| **Dados/gráficos** | Explique números e visuais | "As you can see in this chart, sales grew by 15%." |
| **Conclusão** | Resuma os pontos principais | "To sum up, the key takeaways are..." |
| **Perguntas** | Abra para perguntas | "I'd be happy to take any questions now." |

### 3. Transições úteis dentro da apresentação

- Moving on to the next slide...
- This leads us to an important point...
- Let's take a closer look at...
- As I mentioned earlier...
- On the other hand...
- To put it simply...
- Just to give you some context...

### 4. Lidando com perguntas difíceis

> "That's a great question. Let me address that."
> "I don't have that data with me right now, but I'll follow up with you after the meeting."
> "That's outside the scope of today's presentation, but I'd be happy to discuss it separately."

### 5. Resolvendo imprevistos (nervosismo, esquecimento, problemas técnicos)

> "Sorry, let me just gather my thoughts for a second."
> "Apologies, I seem to be having some technical difficulties, bear with me."
> "Let me rephrase that."

## Vocabulário: Reuniões e apresentações

| Inglês | Português |
|---|---|
| agenda | pauta |
| action items | itens de ação/próximos passos |
| minutes (of the meeting) | ata da reunião |
| stakeholders | partes interessadas |
| takeaway | conclusão/aprendizado principal |
| Q&A (question and answer) | sessão de perguntas e respostas |
| slide deck | apresentação de slides |
| to circle back | retomar um assunto depois |
| to table a discussion | adiar uma discussão |

## Diálogo modelo: Reunião

> **A:** Thanks everyone for joining. Today's agenda includes the Q3 results and next steps for the new campaign.
> **B:** Before we start, can we quickly circle back to last week's action items?
> **A:** Sure, let's do that first. Then I'll hand it over to Ana to walk us through the numbers.
> **C:** Thanks. So, as you can see in this chart, revenue grew by 18% compared to last quarter.
> **B:** That's great. I have a question, what drove that growth specifically?
> **C:** That's a great question. It was mainly driven by our new product launch in July.

## Exercícios

**1)** Complete com a expressão correta (circle back, hand it over, action items, take questions):

a) I'll ▁▁▁ to Pedro for the financial update.
b) Let's ▁▁▁ to that topic after the break.
c) Here's a summary of our ▁▁▁ from today.
d) I'd be happy to ▁▁▁ now.

**2)** Escreva a abertura de uma apresentação sobre um tema de sua escolha (2-3 frases), incluindo a estrutura ("I've divided this into...").

**3)** Traduza:

a) Vamos retomar esse assunto depois. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
b) Essa é uma ótima pergunta. → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁
c) Para resumir, os principais pontos são... → ▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁▁

## Gabarito

**1)** a) hand it over; b) circle back; c) action items; d) take questions

**2)** Resposta livre, exemplo: "Good afternoon, everyone. Today I'll be talking about our expansion plans for next year. I've divided this presentation into three parts: market analysis, budget, and timeline."

**3)** a) Let's circle back to that later. b) That's a great question. c) To sum up, the key points are...

Trabalho · Lição 6: Telefonemas e Videochamadas

Objetivos da lição

  • Atender e fazer ligações profissionais em inglês
  • Lidar com problemas técnicos em videochamadas
  • Pedir para repetir/esclarecer sem constrangimento

Explicação

1. Atendendo o telefone profissionalmente

"Good morning, this is [Name] speaking, how can I help you?" "[Company name], this is [Name] speaking." "Thank you for calling [Company], how may I assist you?"

2. Fazendo uma ligação

"Hi, this is [Name] calling from [Company]. Is this a good time to talk?" "I'm calling regarding the proposal I sent last week." "Could I speak to [Name], please?"

3. Frases essenciais para quando você não entende (fundamentais!)

Não tenha vergonha de pedir para repetir, é muito mais profissional do que fingir que entendeu:

Situação Frase
Não ouviu bem "Sorry, could you repeat that, please?"
Não entendeu o sentido "Could you clarify what you mean by that?"
Precisa que falem mais devagar "Would you mind speaking a bit more slowly, please?"
Quer confirmar que entendeu "So, if I understood correctly, you're saying that...?"
Conexão ruim "Sorry, you're breaking up a little, could you say that again?"
Vai verificar e retornar "Let me check on that and get back to you."

4. Transferindo chamadas e colocando em espera

"One moment, please, I'll transfer you to the right department." "Could you hold for a moment, please?" "I'm sorry, he's not available right now. Would you like to leave a message?" "Can I take a message?"

5. Videochamadas: problemas técnicos comuns

"Sorry, I think you're on mute." "Can everyone see my screen okay?" "I'm having some connection issues, can you hear me now?" "Sorry, we lost you for a second there." "Let's give it a minute for everyone to join."

6. Encerrando uma ligação/call educadamente

"Well, I think that covers everything. Thanks for your time." "I'll send a follow-up email with the details we discussed." "Thanks for calling, have a great day!" "Great, talk soon!"

Diálogo modelo: Ligação profissional

A: Good morning, this is Fernanda speaking from Global Tech, how can I help you? B: Hi Fernanda, this is Marcus calling from ABC Solutions. Is this a good time to talk about the contract? A: Sure, go ahead. B: Great. So, regarding the terms we discussed, sorry, you're breaking up a little, could you say that again? A: No problem, let me try calling you back, the connection seems unstable.

Diálogo modelo: Videochamada

A: Hi everyone, can you all hear me okay? B: Yes, loud and clear. But I think Carlos is on mute. C: Sorry about that, can you hear me now? A: Perfect. Let's get started, I'll share my screen now.

Exercícios

1) Complete com a frase adequada (breaking up, on mute, get back to you, transfer you):

a) Sorry, you're , could you repeat that? b) I think you're , we can't hear you. c) Let me check and later today. d) One moment, I'll to the sales department.

2) Traduza:

a) Poderia falar um pouco mais devagar, por favor? → b) Ele não está disponível. Gostaria de deixar um recado? → c) Vocês conseguem ver minha tela? →

Gabarito

1) a) breaking up; b) on mute; c) get back to you; d) transfer you

2) a) Would you mind speaking a bit more slowly, please? b) He's not available. Would you like to leave a message? c) Can everyone see my screen?

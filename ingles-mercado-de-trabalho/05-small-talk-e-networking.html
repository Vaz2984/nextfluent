Trabalho · Lição 5: Small Talk e Networking

Objetivos da lição

  • Fazer conversa informal (small talk) no ambiente de trabalho
  • Se apresentar e construir relacionamentos em eventos de networking
  • Saber quais temas evitar em conversas profissionais

Explicação

1. Por que small talk importa no trabalho?

Em culturas de língua inglesa (especialmente EUA, Reino Unido, Austrália), o small talk é parte essencial de construir relacionamentos profissionais, antes de reuniões, no elevador, em eventos. Não é "perda de tempo": é como se cria confiança.

2. Temas seguros para small talk

Tema Exemplo de pergunta
Fim de semana "Did you have a good weekend? Any plans?"
Clima "Can you believe this weather we're having?"
Trabalho (leve) "How's everything going with the new project?"
Viagens "Have you traveled anywhere interesting recently?"
Hobbies "Do you have any hobbies outside of work?"
Elogios genuínos "I really liked your presentation, by the way."

3. Temas para EVITAR em contexto profissional (especialmente com pessoas que você não conhece bem)

  • Salário de outras pessoas
  • Política e religião
  • Peso, aparência física, idade (especialmente perguntar a idade de alguém)
  • Vida amorosa/relacionamentos pessoais detalhados
  • Fofocas sobre colegas

4. Frases para começar e manter uma conversa

Iniciando:

"Hi, I don't think we've met, I'm [Name] from the marketing team." "Mind if I join you?" "This is a great event, isn't it?"

Mantendo a conversa (fazendo perguntas de acompanhamento):

"That's interesting, how did you get into that?" "What brings you to this conference?" "What do you do at [Company]?"

Encerrando educadamente:

"It was great talking to you, I should go say hi to a few other people, but let's stay in touch." "I don't want to keep you, but it was a pleasure meeting you!"

5. Networking em eventos profissionais

Se apresentando (elevator pitch, resumo de 30 segundos):

"Hi, I'm Beatriz, I work as a UX designer at a fintech startup. I focus mostly on improving onboarding experiences for new users. What about you?"

Trocando contato:

"It was great meeting you. Do you have a card, or should we connect on LinkedIn?" "Let's exchange contacts, I'd love to keep in touch."

Fazendo follow-up depois do evento (por e-mail ou LinkedIn):

"It was great meeting you at the conference yesterday. I really enjoyed our conversation about [topic]. Let's stay in touch!"

Vocabulário: Small talk e networking

Inglês Português

Diálogo modelo: Networking event

A: Hi, mind if I join you? I don't think we've met, I'm Thiago. B: Not at all! I'm Laura, nice to meet you. What brings you to this event? A: I'm here to learn more about the latest trends in fintech. What do you do? B: I work as a product manager at a payments company. What about you? A: I'm a software developer, actually looking for new opportunities in this space. B: Oh, that's great, we're actually hiring! Let's connect on LinkedIn, I'd be happy to introduce you to our recruiter.
